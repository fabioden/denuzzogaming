Concierge AI for the hospitality sector

A virtual chat assistant that answers customers around the clock, in Italian and English, about opening hours, menu, reservations and events — drawing on the business's real data.

Role: Design & development Type: AI Agent · Web widget Sector: Hospitality
The context

Endless questions, at all hours

A hospitality business receives dozens of repetitive messages every day: "Are you open tonight?", "Do you have vegetarian options?", "How do I book a table?", "Do you host private events?". Often outside business hours, when no one is around to reply — and every missed answer is a customer (or a booking) lost.

The solution

A digital concierge that never sleeps

I designed and built an AI assistant integrated into the website: it answers customers' questions on its own, naturally and in the brand's tone of voice. It knows the business's real data and, when a piece of information isn't available (e.g. specific allergens or details about private events), it doesn't make things up: it politely refers the customer to the staff. The result: the customer gets an answer right away, and the staff is freed from repetitive questions.

How it works

Four pieces, one seamless experience

01

Knowledge base

The business's real data (opening hours, menu, rules, events) is structured into a knowledge base that the agent draws on.

02

AI engine (Claude)

An advanced language model interprets the question and crafts the answer in the brand's tone of voice, in IT/EN.

03

Secure backend

A serverless function acts as a bridge: the AI access key stays protected on the server, never exposed to the browser.

04

Website widget

A lightweight chat, embeddable in any site without slowing it down, ready to answer day and night.
